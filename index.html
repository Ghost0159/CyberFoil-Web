CyberFoil

Nintendo Switch homebrew installer. NSP, NSZ, XCI, and XCZ from SD, LAN, Remote, USB, or MTP. Themes, custom offline packs, and save or cheat sync when the server speaks AeroFoil.

Works with Atmosphere and other custom firmware.
